---
title: "Singing Me Home"
summary: "A poem about finding one's way back through music and memory"
status: "Published"
excerpt: "The melody carries me through the night, a familiar tune that guides my way home."
category: "Personal"
---

The melody carries me through the night,
a familiar tune that guides my way home.
Each note a memory, each chord a light,
illuminating paths I've learned to roam.

Through city streets and country lanes,
the music echoes in my mind.
It calls to me through joys and pains,
a constant companion, always kind.

The rhythm matches my heartbeat,
steady and strong, never to fade.
It's the song that makes me complete,
the harmony that keeps me unafraid.

When I'm lost in the darkness,
when the world seems too much to bear,
the music finds me in my weakness,
and reminds me that I'm almost there.

Home isn't just a place to rest,
it's the feeling in my chest.
It's the song that never ends,
the melody that makes amends.

So I follow the sound,
let it lead me where I belong.
For in its sweet, familiar ground,
I find the strength to carry on.

The music is calling,
singing me home.
Through every fall and every rising,
it's the anthem of my soul.

Our story begins in Washoe Valley, Nevada.
Eyes bright at the tender age of four,
My mother brought a man into our lives.
A shadow I couldn’t simply ignore.

Hiding behind my grandmother, I sensed danger,
The intuition of a child, a sign of warning.
I wanted him gone, a feeling raw and true.
Unaware of the storm, slowly coming to shore.

We moved to Phoenix, Arizona.
At ﬁrst, things were great,
We were happy, life was divine.
Learning to read and write,
Finding joy in fantasy and games.

At seven, we moved to Queen Creek.
A new place, the start of a dark story.
The shadow of the storm,
ﬁnally reaching shore.

The shadow started training me,
Recognizing aptitude in the martial arts.
The nightmare began to unfold,
Wave by battering wave the curtain fell.

His cruelty masked in everyday life,
A dark secret my mother tried to hide.
Yet I saw, I knew,
The abuse inﬂicted, hidden yet clear.

To my mother and me, his tyranny grew,
In shadows and whispers, always nearby.
For her it came in the shadow of night,
After the cursed drink had been consumed.

For me, it came in isolation,
When he couldn’t be caught.
Mother was gone,
And the shadow would have “conversations”

He did not know he was training a warrior,
For that alone I will thank him.
Teaching me what to stand for what I believe in,
To protect those who need defending.
